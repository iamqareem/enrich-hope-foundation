About Us

Growth needs a hand to hold.

Enrich Hope Foundation is a Kampala-based charity dedicated to lifting up individuals and families through compassionate, community-led programs. Every initiative we run — from classrooms to clinics — is built on the belief that hope, given the right support, takes root and grows.

We work hand in hand with local leaders, volunteers, and donors to build solutions that last well beyond a single donation.

Our Vision

A world where everyone has the opportunity to live with dignity, purpose, and hope.

What We Do

Our Programs

Education & Mentorship

Scholarships, school supplies, and mentorship that keep children in the classroom and on a path forward.

Health & Wellbeing

Community health outreach, maternal support, and access to basic care for families who need it most.

Community Development

Livelihood training and small-business support that help communities become self-sustaining.

Emergency Relief

Rapid, practical support — food, shelter, and essentials — for families facing sudden hardship.

Mosque Construction

Building and renovating mosques that serve as centers of worship, learning, and community gathering.

Borehole Drilling

Drilling boreholes and installing hand pumps to bring safe, reliable drinking water into underserved communities.
