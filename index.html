About the Foundation

Rooted in Kampala.
Reaching the Nations.

Enrich Hope Foundation is a registered charity dedicated to lifting up individuals through community-led programs. Every initiative we run—from classrooms to clinics—is built on the belief that hope, given the right support, takes root and grows.

Impact Areas

Sustainable Programs

Education & Mentorship

Keeping children in school with supplies, tuition support, and life-skills mentorship.

Health & Wellbeing

Community health outreach and maternal support for families in underserved areas.

Community Development

Clean water projects and livelihood training to create self-sustaining communities.
